Gaden demo: a hunger circuit with a controller

A one-day simulation that runs in your browser (WebAssembly, 80 KB, no libraries). Move a slider and the day is simulated again.

Simulation only. This is a computer model. It is not a medical device, not tested in animals or people, and not medical advice. Do not use it to make decisions about food, sleep, drugs or treatment.

Inputs

Meals are grams of carbohydrate. Sleep is hours in bed. Gain error scales how strongly current changes the firing rate.
